Questions before you download

Do I need a payment card or a license key?

No. The appliance starts a 30-day local trial on first boot. A registration key is needed only to receive signed product updates, and you can request one during the evaluation.

Where does my monitoring data go?

It stays with you. ZenPlus runs entirely on the appliance inside your network, and your monitoring data remains within your infrastructure.

Which hypervisors are supported?

This release is published for VMware Workstation 17 or later and Microsoft Hyper-V (Generation 2). For VMware ESXi, KVM, Proxmox or Nutanix, tell us your platform and we will confirm the right package.

Why do you ask for my details?

So that a real engineer can help with your evaluation, and so we know who is running our software. We do not sell or share your details. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens when the trial ends?

Talk to us before the 30 days are over and we will confirm the subscription that fits your estate. See pricing and trial and licensing.

I am already a customer. Do I need this download?

No. Keep your appliance and update it from Settings. Use this download only for a new installation.
